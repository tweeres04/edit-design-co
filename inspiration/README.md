## What's in this folder

- `contact-sheets/`: each homepage top to bottom, one image per site
- `screenshots/`: individual viewport frames, `-0` is above the fold
- `styles/`: computed fonts, sizes, colours, nav links and headings pulled from each homepage

## Common to all six

1. **Photography is the design.** A large, often full-bleed interior photo opens the page, usually with no text on it (Ayume is the exception). Photos are warm, naturally lit and styled: flowers, books, ceramics, wood grain, textiles.
2. **Warm, earthy palette taken from the photos.** Off-white, cream, putty or pale grey backgrounds instead of pure white (Yond `#D7D0B2`, W Design `#FFFFFA`, Pamela `#F2F2F2`). Accents come from the photos (olive, caramel, dark brown). No saturated brand colour.
3. **Very little copy.** A one-sentence statement of approach, then images.
4. **Small, quiet navigation.** 4 to 7 links at 13–17px, logo on the left. Labels: Projects, About, Services, Process, Contact/Inquire.
5. **Small, light body type.** 13–17px, often light weight with slight tracking.
6. **Wordmark logos.** The name set in type, no icon.
7. **Restrained layout.** Lots of whitespace, no cards, no shadows, no rounded buttons. Buttons are thin outlined rectangles or underlined links.
8. **Project-first structure.** Projects named after the house or place ("Cadboro Bay Beach House", "Yates Penthouse"). Portfolios are image grids.
9. **Minimal footer.** Address, email/phone, Instagram.

## Common to most

- Serif display type paired with a plain sans for body (Yond, W Design, Ayume, Falken). Pamela and Lucien use sans only.
- Founder portrait and bio (Falken, Ayume, Lucien, W Design).
- Location stated up front. Four are BC studios (Falken, Ayume and Lucien in Vancouver, Pamela around Victoria).
- Built on Squarespace (five of six; Falken is the exception).

## Common to some

- Press and awards as credibility (Falken, Yond, Lucien).
- An online shop (Yond, W Design).

## What none of them do

Stock imagery, pricing, loud CTAs, testimonial carousels, heavy animation, bright colours.
